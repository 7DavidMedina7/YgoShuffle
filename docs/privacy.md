# YGO Shuffle Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

YGO Shuffle is developed by David A. Medina. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app's data practices.

## Data stored on your device

YGO Shuffle stores your rule lists, rule text and categories, category filters, selected list, spin settings, and up to 100 completed spin results on your device. History includes the generated rule, source list name and identifiers, and the date and time of the spin. This information is used to provide the app's features, restore your choices, and avoid consecutive duplicate results when that setting is enabled.

The app does not send this information to the developer or operate a server for storing it. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

## Files you choose to import, export, or share

When you select a JSON file in the system file picker, the app reads it to import a rule list. When you export or share a list, the file includes its name, identifiers, rule text, categories, and enabled-category filters. It does not include spin history or spin settings.

You choose where to save exported files and which app or recipient receives a shared file. For example, choosing a cloud drive, email, or messaging service transfers that file to the service you selected. Its own privacy practices apply. Review your custom rules before sharing them if they contain personal information.

## Retention and removal

Local data remains until you change or remove it in the app, or delete the app from your device. Use Rule History → Clear to remove saved history. Deleting a list does not remove its earlier history entries; clear history separately. The app keeps at least one rule list.

Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its local app data. Files you saved or sent elsewhere remain in those locations until you delete them there. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings; the app does not provide its own cloud synchronization or backup service.

## External pages and support

Opening Privacy Policy or Support uses your browser to visit GitHub. GitHub may process information about your visit according to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

If you submit a GitHub issue, the developer can see the information you choose to include, and that issue is public. Do not post personal information, passwords, or private rule-list files. Support reports are used to respond to questions and investigate problems; the app does not automatically send a support report.

Apple may process App Store, device-backup, or diagnostic information under its own settings and policies. This policy describes the app's own behavior.

## Children

The app does not have a separate data-collection feature for children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. Avoid including personal information in public support reports.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.

For questions about the app or this policy, see the [support page](support.md) or [contact the developer through GitHub Issues](https://github.com/7DavidMedina7/YgoShuffle/issues/new).

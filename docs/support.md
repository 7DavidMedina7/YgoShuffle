# YGO Shuffle Support

YGO Shuffle is an iPhone and iPad companion app that generates random house rules for card-game duels. It is developed by David A. Medina and is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Konami.

## Contact

[Ask a question or report a problem on GitHub](https://github.com/7DavidMedina7/YgoShuffle/issues/new).

GitHub requires an account to submit an issue. Reports are public. Include the app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are helpful when they contain no personal information. Never include passwords, payment details, or private files.

## Common questions

### How do I play?

Open How to Play in the app. At the start of each player's Standby Phase, spin and apply the generated house-rule effect. This is an unofficial variant, not an official tournament format.

### Why can't I spin?

Open Filters and enable a category containing rules, or use Enable All Categories. Make sure the active list contains rules. Wait for any current spin to finish.

### How do I customize rules?

Open Lists, select a list, and add or edit rules and their categories. Add List creates another list. Press and hold a list to duplicate it or share its JSON file.

### How do I move a list to another device?

In Lists, press and hold a list and choose Share JSON or Save JSON File. On the other device, choose Import Rule List and select that JSON file. Imports create a new list; they do not replace an existing list. Files must be no larger than 1 MB and contain no more than 1,000 rules. Spin history and settings are not included.

### Why are haptics or sound missing?

Check Settings. Sound starts disabled and respects silent mode. Haptics require a supported device and are generally unavailable on iPad. Quick Spin changes animation timing; Reduce Motion changes the reel animation.

### Where is my data?

Rule lists, history, and preferences are saved locally. The app has no account or automatic cross-device synchronization. Export important rule lists before deleting the app. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

### Can I clear history?

Open History → Clear and confirm. Clearing history also resets consecutive-duplicate comparisons. Deleting a list does not clear its historical results.
